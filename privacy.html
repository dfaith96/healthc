What this policy covers

This policy describes how HealthComm handles information submitted through this website. We aim to collect only what is reasonably needed to respond to inquiries and improve the site.

Information you provide

When you use the contact form, you may choose to provide your name, email address, organization or role, and message. Please do not include sensitive medical information in the contact form.

How information is used

We use submitted information to respond to your request, communicate about HealthComm, and maintain the safety and quality of our services. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing and retention

Information may be shared with service providers who help operate this site or where required by law. We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes described above or as required by law.

Your choices

You can request access, correction, or deletion of personal information by contacting hello@healthcomm.it.com. We may need to verify your request before responding.

Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will appear on this page with a revised date.
